Direct Answer: How Gas Furnace Repair Works

Gas furnace repair involves troubleshooting the sequential combustion process: thermostat call → draft inducer motor start → negative pressure switch closure → hot surface igniter glow → gas valve opening → flame rectification proof → blower activation. If any safety switch detects improper venting, low gas pressure, or lack of flame, the control board halts the cycle to prevent gas accumulation. High-Intent Sub-Services & Key Components We Target

  • Hot Surface Igniter (HSI) Replacement: Silicon carbide and silicon nitride igniters withstand thousands of heating cycles but eventually crack from thermal stress. When the igniter fails to glow white-hot, gas will not ignite.
  • Flame Sensor Cleaning & Rectification: Over time, microscopic silica and carbon particles coat the stainless-steel flame rod. The furnace board cannot detect the flame's electrical microamp signal and cuts gas after 3 seconds. Cleaning or replacing this sensor restores instant operation.
  • Combination Gas Valve Diagnostics: The solenoid-driven gas valve regulates pressure down to 3.5 inches of water column (in. w.c.). If the 24V coil burns out or the internal diaphragm sticks, gas flow is interrupted.
  • Pressure Switch & Draft Inducer Repair: High-efficiency gas furnaces use a draft inducer blower to purge exhaust fumes. If the flue is blocked or the pressure switch diaphragm fails, safety lockouts prevent burner ignition.
  • Flame Rollout & Limit Switches: Safety thermal switches shut down fuel supply if flames roll outside the combustion chamber or if restricted airflow creates attic heat buildup.

Signs Your Gas Furnace Requires Professional Attention

Clicking Without Ignition

You hear rapid clicking from the furnace followed by silence. The control board attempted ignition, failed three times, and engaged a safety lockout.

Delayed Ignition "Boom"

Gas accumulates in the burner chamber for a few seconds before violently igniting, creating a loud bang that can crack your heat exchanger.

Discolored Yellow Burner Flames

Indicates incomplete combustion and excessive carbon monoxide production. Burner orifices must be thoroughly cleaned and calibrated.

Continuous Blower with Cold Air

The safety limit switch has tripped due to internal overheating, keeping the blower running to cool the heat exchanger while blocking fuel flow.

Gas Furnace Diagnostic & Repair Workflow

When you place a call to our dispatch center, here is what you can expect during the on-site visit:

  1. Visual & Leak Inspection: Technicians inspect the incoming black-iron gas pipe, shutoff cock, and flex connector using electronic gas sniffers.
  2. Ignition Circuit Diagnostic: Testing line voltage and 24V transformer step-down output to ensure proper voltage reaches the ignition control module.
  3. Manifold Gas Pressure Calibration: Attaching a digital dual-port manometer to the gas valve to verify correct fuel-to-air combustion mixture.
  4. Burner Assembly Servicing: Removing in-shot burners to clear rust, debris, and insect nests that accumulate during humid Texas summers.
  5. Flue Gas & Draft Testing: Measuring draft in the flue pipe to guarantee all carbon monoxide exhaust vents completely outside the home.
