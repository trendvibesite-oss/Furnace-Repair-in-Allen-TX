Common Heating Complexities in Star Creek Homes

  • Condensate Drain Freezes: 90%+ AFUE condensing furnaces extract heat from water vapor, creating acidic condensation that drains via exterior PVC lines. When sub-freezing Texas cold fronts hit, uninsulated drain lines freeze solid, backing water up and tripping the condensate float safety switch.
  • ECM Blower Motor Controller Surges: North Texas power grid fluctuations during severe winter storms can damage the sensitive microprocessor module attached to variable-speed blower motors.
  • Communicating Thermostat Signal Drops: Smart thermostat communication buses (ABCD wiring) can experience data packet drops if 24V transformers experience voltage sags.
  • Static Pressure Airflow Trips: Using ultra-dense 1-inch allergy filters chokes the variable-speed motor, causing the control board to throttle down or lock out on internal high temperature limits.
