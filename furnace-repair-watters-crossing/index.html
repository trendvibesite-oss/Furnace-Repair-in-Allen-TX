Common Furnace Breakdowns in Watters Crossing

  • Blower Motor Bearing Wear: Decades of continuous operation cause permanent split capacitor (PSC) blower motor sleeve bearings to run dry, resulting in loud screeching and high amperage draw.
  • Cracked Clamshell Heat Exchangers: Repetitive thermal stress over 20+ years causes micro-fractures in metal heat exchanger cells, necessitating immediate optical inspection and carbon monoxide testing.
  • Corroded Gas Burner Ports: Attic dust and humidity corrode steel in-shot burner orifices, leading to delayed gas ignition bangs and yellow, carbon-monoxide-producing flames.
  • Failing Electronic Ignition Controls: Early intermittent pilot modules and silicon carbide igniters lose calibration and fail to light burners reliably during sudden cold fronts.
