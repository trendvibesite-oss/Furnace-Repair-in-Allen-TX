Common Thermostat Problems We Diagnose

Blank or Unresponsive Screen

Caused by dead backup batteries, an open high-limit safety float switch cutting 24V power, or a blown low-voltage fuse on the furnace board.

Furnace Not Responding to Heat Calls

Corroded terminal contacts, severed low-voltage control wiring in attic spaces, or failing heating relays prevent signal transmission.

Continuous Short-Cycling

Incorrect heat anticipator settings or poor thermostat placement near exterior drafts causes erratic cycling and temperature swings.

Smart Thermostat Wi-Fi Disconnects

Inadequate power delivery from missing C-wire connections causes smart thermostats to drain internal batteries and reboot during heating calls.
