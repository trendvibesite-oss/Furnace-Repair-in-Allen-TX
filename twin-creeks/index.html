Common Furnace Challenges in Twin Creeks Residences

  • Attic Thermal Stress: During Texas summers, Twin Creeks attics can reach 140°F, which degrades plastic pressure switch vacuum tubes, motor capacitors, and wiring harnesses before winter even begins.
  • Dual-Unit Synchronization: When an upstairs furnace fails, the downstairs furnace attempts to compensate, running non-stop and frequently overheating on its high-limit switch.
  • Motorized Zone Damper Malfunctions: Many larger properties feature automated duct dampers. If an actuator motor burns out, conditioned heat cannot reach specific bedroom wings.
  • Combustion Air Restriction: Attic insulation blown during recent energy upgrades can inadvertently obstruct combustion air intake vents, causing burner flame rollouts.
