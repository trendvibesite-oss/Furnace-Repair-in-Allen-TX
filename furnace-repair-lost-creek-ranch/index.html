Common Furnace Issues in Lost Creek Ranch Homes

  • Dirty Flame Sensor Safety Lockouts: After months of summer inactivity, thin oxidation layers prevent the flame sensor rod from transmitting microamps to the control board, shutting gas off after 3 seconds.
  • High-Limit Thermal Trips: Family households often experience heavy dust and pet hair buildup. If return air filters are not changed frequently, airflow across the heat exchanger drops, tripping the primary high-limit disc.
  • Mud Dauber & Insect Flue Obstructions: During hot Collin County summers, mud daubers frequently build nests inside exterior furnace exhaust flue caps or pressure switch tubing, preventing negative draft switches from closing in winter.
  • Blower Fan Run Capacitor Weakness: When motor capacitors drop below their rated microfarads (µF), the blower fan fails to start, causing the furnace to overheat within two minutes.
