Common Heating Complexities in Montgomery Farm Homes

  • Heat Pump Defrost Failures: When North Texas freezes produce freezing rain and sleet, the outdoor coil naturally frosts over. If the defrost control board or sensor fails, the unit encases itself in ice and shuts down on thermal overload.
  • Burnt Auxiliary Heat Strips: When outdoor temperatures drop into the 20s, electric resistance backup coils activate. If nichrome coils snap or staging sequencers fail, the home loses secondary heating capacity. Learn more on our electric furnace repair page.
  • Tripped 60-Amp Electrical Breakers: Auxiliary heating packs draw substantial electrical current (up to 40-50 amps). Loose wire lugs or failing contactors cause breaker trips.
  • Smart Energy Zone Damper Glitches: Eco-homes with multi-zone duct configurations depend on automated damper actuators. If an actuator sticks, certain living zones are deprived of heat.
