Our Multi-Point Diagnostic Testing Protocol

When an independent technician arrives at your Allen property, they execute a comprehensive step-by-step diagnostic checklist:

  1. Control Board & Flash Code Retrieval: Reading stored fault code history from the integrated furnace control (IFC) module via LED flash pulses.
  2. Thermostat & Low-Voltage Circuit Testing: Verifying 24VAC across terminal strips (R, W, G, Y, C) to ensure the thermostat is communicating heat demands without voltage drops.
  3. Safety Circuit Continuity Testing: Testing resistance across all series-wired safety switches: rollout switches, high-limit thermal discs, and auxiliary limits.
  4. Draft Inducer & Flue Static Pressure: Measuring negative pressure in inches of water column to ensure combustion gases are safely evacuated.
  5. Ignition Component Bench Testing: Measuring electrical resistance (ohms) across the hot surface igniter and reading microamps generated by the flame sensor.
  6. Blower Motor & Capacitor Analysis: Checking capacitor ratings against rated µF specifications and verifying motor winding temperatures.
  7. Heat Exchanger Visual & Combustible Gas Check: Inspecting burner cells for cracks, rust perforations, or carbon soot deposits that could release carbon monoxide.
